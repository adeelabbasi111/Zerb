Engineering Discipline

How we build games & mechanics.

01

Mechanics-First Prototyping

Before writing complex shaders or lore, we build greybox prototypes to validate the core loop. If the core interaction isn't immediately satisfying, no amount of graphics will save it.

02

Deterministic Physics

We isolate game physics from variable frame rates, ensuring collisions, ballistic trajectories, and movement speeds behave identically on every device.

03

Shader & Draw Call Tuning

We batch draw calls, optimize geometry LODs, and author custom shaders that maintain steady 60fps on mobile and 90fps in virtual reality headsets.

04

Polished Juice & Feedback

Screen shake, particle bursts, spatial audio triggers, and tactile haptics are tuned together to give every user action weight, impact, and reward.
